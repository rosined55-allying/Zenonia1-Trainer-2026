## 🎮 About the Game

ZENONIA 1 is a single-player action role-playing game developed by GAMEVIL and first released in 2009. It follows Regret through a fantasy adventure involving quests, dungeon exploration, real-time combat, equipment upgrades, and character development.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for ZENONIA 1 by modifying common character and progression values. It is designed for players who want easier combat, faster development, more resources, or greater flexibility while exploring the game's quests, towns, dungeons, equipment, skills, and real-time battles.

**Target Audience:** This trainer is intended for players who have completed or want to simplify ZENONIA 1, experiment with character builds, reduce repetitive grinding, revisit the story, or focus on quests and exploration rather than resource management and difficult combat.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Keeps the character's health from being depleted during combat.
- **Unlimited Mana** — Prevents mana from decreasing when using skills and other mana-consuming abilities.
- **Unlimited Gold** — Provides a practically unlimited supply of gold for purchases, equipment, supplies, and other expenses.
- **Add Experience** — Accelerates character progression by increasing experience gained from combat and quests.
- **Add Skill Points** — Provides additional skill points for improving the character's available abilities.
- **Maximum Character Stats** — Improves core character attributes to make progression and encounters easier.
- **Unlimited Consumables** — Allows health potions, mana potions, and other usable supplies to remain available during an adventure.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts the character's movement speed for quicker travel through towns, fields, and dungeons.
- **Easy Equipment Management** — Makes it easier to maintain a strong loadout while experimenting with weapons, armor, accessories, and other equipment.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Kills** — Defeats ordinary enemies with a single successful attack.
- **Unlimited Skill Use** — Removes resource limitations that normally restrict repeated use of combat skills.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Increases outgoing damage to shorten battles against enemies and dungeon bosses.
- **Defense Multiplier** — Reduces incoming damage and improves survivability during real-time encounters.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Stops enemy actions temporarily while leaving the character free to move and attack.
- **No Cooldowns** — Removes waiting periods from supported skills and combat actions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the game's normal movement controls to travel through towns, outdoor areas, and dungeons. The movement speed option can make long journeys faster, but it should be disabled during scripted sequences or when entering a new area if movement behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Gold can be used to purchase equipment, consumables, and other supplies from vendors. Keep the inventory organized by selling unwanted gear and reserving useful potions for difficult encounters. Unlimited consumables and equipment-related options are best used after the relevant item has already been obtained normally.

### World & Environment

ZENONIA 1 combines towns, fields, dungeons, NPC interactions, quests, shops, and story events. Explore each area, speak with available characters, and complete objectives to advance the adventure. Save the game regularly before activating progression-related options or entering major story locations.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat takes place in real time and combines basic attacks, movement, equipment bonuses, and character skills. Manage health and mana during encounters, use skills against groups or stronger enemies, and adjust the trainer's damage, defense, one-hit-kill, or enemy-control options to match the desired difficulty. Avoid stacking every combat option at once if you want equipment and character development to remain meaningful.
